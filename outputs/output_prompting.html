- **Carbs**: 1-1.5g per kg body weight = **75-112g**

  - Rice cakes with honey
- **Protein**: 15-20g (aids satiety)
- **Hydration**: 400-600ml water
- **Avoid**: High fiber, high fat (causes GI distress)

## During Ride (every 45 min)

  - Energy gels or chews

- **Hydration**: 150-250ml every 15-20 min

## Post-Ride (within 30 mins)
- **Carbs**: 1-1.2g per kg = **75-90g**
- **Protein**: 20-25g
- **Example**: 
  - Lentil soup with bread
  - Greek yogurt with granola and berries
  - Veggie sandwich with cheese
- **Hydration**: 500-750ml with electrolytes

## Hydration Summary
- Total: ~1.5-2L over session (depending on conditions/sweat rate)
